## Azulle Quantum Byte

- CPU: Intel Atom Z3735F (Bay Trail-T), quad-core 1.33 GHz, burst 1.83 GHz
- GPU: Intel HD Graphics
- RAM: 2 GB DDR3L
- Storage: 32 GB eMMC, expandable via card reader
- OS: Windows 10, fanless, 12 V / 1 A power
- Ports include VGA, LAN, card reader, audio jack

## Dell P2314T

- 1920 × 1080 at 60 Hz, IPS, 0.265 mm pixel pitch
- 10-point touch, fingers or thin gloves, <10 ms touch response
- Inputs: DisplayPort 1.2, HDMI/MHL 2.0, VGA
- Touch data goes over USB: the monitor's USB upstream cable must be connected to the PC, or the screen displays but doesn't respond to touch
